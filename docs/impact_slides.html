SME Tender-Win

Democratizing Public Procurement through AI Agents.

Hackathon Submission 2026

The Problem

Information Gap

SMEs miss high-value government contracts because they can't monitor multiple portals 24/7.

Resource Gap

Professional bid writing is expensive. SMEs can't afford full-time procurement experts.

The Solution

An autonomous agent that handles the "Top of Funnel" procurement work:

Discover

Automated scraping of government tenders via Firecrawl.

Filter

AI-driven 'Win Probability' based on SME capabilities.

Draft

Instant technical approach drafts and compliance checklists.

Scale

Allows a 10-person team to compete with 1000-person firms.

Business Impact

Efficiency

Drafting time reduced from 40 hours to 4 minutes.

Accuracy

100% coverage of niche-relevant tenders.

Result: Higher revenue for SMEs, more innovation for the Government.
