# Demo Video Script: SME Tender-Win (2-3 Minutes)

## Scene 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:30)
**Visual:** Screen recording of a complex government tender portal (lots of tables, PDFs).
**Audio:** "For most small businesses, government contracts are a goldmine, but the gates are locked. They are locked by complex portals, endless PDFs, and the need for a full-time procurement team that most SMEs simply can't afford."

## Scene 2: Introducing the Agent (0:30 - 1:00)
**Visual:** Show the `sme_profile.json` and then the terminal running `python src/main.py`.
**Audio:** "Introducing SME Tender-Win. We start by defining the SME's DNA—their certifications, niche, and past wins. Then, the agent takes over. Using Firecrawl, it monitors government portals in real-time, searching for exactly what the company does best."

## Scene 3: The 'Magic' Moment (1:00 - 2:00)
**Visual:** Terminal output showing the "Win Probability: 75%" and the "Justification" being printed. Then show the "Technical Approach" draft.
**Audio:** "Here's the magic. Instead of a human spending hours reading a 50-page tender, the agent analyzes it instantly. It calculates a 'Win Probability.' In this case, 75%. It tells the business owner exactly why they are a fit and where the gaps are. Then, it doesn't just stop at analysis—it drafts the initial technical approach and creates a checklist of every document needed for the bid."

## Scene 4: The Closing/Impact (2:00 - 3:00)
**Visual:** Transition to the Impact Slides (HTML).
**Audio:** "SME Tender-Win turns a week of manual work into a few seconds of AI processing. We are democratizing procurement, allowing small, innovative firms to compete on a level playing field with industry giants. That is the power of SME-tailored AI."
